Pricing versus Valuation: Across the life cycle I will start with a distinction that I believe needs to be made between two approaches to attaching numbers to assets and businesses.

The first is to value that asset or business, based on its capacity to generate and grow cash flows, and the risk in those cashflows, i.e., intrinsic valuation.
